Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does local SEO take?

Some improvements move quickly, but competitive markets usually take several months of consistent work. Our initial plan covers the first 90 days, and we measure the same map grid throughout so you can see what's actually shifting.

Can you guarantee top-three rankings?

No, and nobody honestly can. A position on Google depends on location, competition and relevance, none of which any agency controls. What we'll guarantee is the thing you actually care about. Where the audit shows the gaps we usually find, we'll commit in writing to doubling the calls and enquiries you get from Google within 90 days, and keep working at no charge until we do.

How does the guarantee work?

It's offered case by case, after the audit, and only where the numbers support it. If your Google Maps presence is already in the top 10–20% we can still help you, but we won't be able to offer the guarantee. We document your current call volume before any work starts and install call tracking on day one, so the baseline isn't a guess. Your side is the same deal the guarantee section lists, with numbers attached: [15] reviews through our system in the first 90 days, [10] new job photos a month from around your service area, answering the phone when leads call, and access to your profile and website within [7] days of starting. If we miss the target with those in place, we keep working for free until we hit it.

Do I need to attend a call?

No. We analyse your business, send your video audit and proposal, and run the campaign entirely by email. If you'd rather talk things through, you can book a call after you apply. It's an option, never a requirement.

Will I need a new website?

Usually not. We may recommend changes to your existing site, or additional service and location pages. Anything substantial is scoped and priced in the proposal before you approve it.

Is this just Google Business Profile optimisation?

No. Your profile is one signal among several. We also work on your website, reviews, citations, local relevance, authority and conversion tracking, because the profile alone rarely moves a competitive market.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. We start with a 90-day program, but nothing locks you into it. Everything is month to month and you're free to leave at any time. That said, if you're not willing to give it at least 90 days, we'd honestly recommend not starting at all. Local SEO doesn't deliver instant results, and 90 days is the fair point to judge whether it's working.
